# Comfort Giggles Underwriting Review

Use this checklist before submitting the site for underwriting review.

## 1. Security
- Confirm the live site loads over HTTPS with no browser warnings.
- Confirm checkout is served over HTTPS and payment details are handled by ComfortPay or another approved payment provider.
- Confirm no test/debug pages, server errors, admin screens, or secrets are publicly exposed.
- Confirm platform dependencies and hosting settings are current.

## 2. User Experience
- Confirm main navigation works: Home, Shop, Our Story, Contact, Cart, Checkout, and policy pages.
- Confirm product discovery, filters, cart, and checkout flow are understandable.
- Confirm contact details are easy to find.
- Confirm there is no placeholder text, broken imagery, or confusing redirect behavior.

## 3. Content Quality
- Confirm product descriptions are original, clear, and consistent with what is sold.
- Confirm claims such as ingredients, hand-poured production, shipping, and fulfillment are accurate.
- Confirm copy is free of spelling, grammar, and encoding issues.
- Confirm About, Contact, policy, product, cart, and checkout pages use consistent business details.

## 4. SEO and SEM
- Confirm page titles and descriptions identify Comfort Giggles and the candle product category.
- Confirm product pages use readable names, URLs, headings, and image alt text.
- Confirm paid ad claims, if any, match the landing page, product pricing, shipping, and refund terms.
- Confirm there is no keyword stuffing, hidden text, or misleading redirect behavior.

## 5. Mobile Responsiveness
- Confirm the homepage, product catalog, product detail pages, cart, checkout, and contact form work on mobile.
- Confirm text is readable without zooming.
- Confirm buttons, menus, forms, and checkout controls are easy to tap.
- Confirm no horizontal scrolling or overlapping content appears on small screens.

## 6. Loading Speed and Performance
- Confirm images are reasonably sized and render quickly.
- Confirm the product catalog and checkout do not show long blank states.
- Confirm there are no recurring console errors in production.
- Confirm Core Web Vitals are acceptable on the live domain.

## 7. Social Media Integration
- Confirm any social profile links are official, active, and consistent with the site brand.
- Confirm business name, logo, contact details, and claims match across linked profiles.
- Confirm linked profiles do not include misleading or prohibited promotional content.

## 8. Backlink Profile
- Confirm business listings use the same business name, phone, email, and address.
- Confirm backlinks are relevant and not from spam, paid-link, or prohibited-content sources.
- Confirm anchor text looks natural and not manipulative.

## 9. Scam Detection
- Confirm business identity is clear: Comfort Giggles, contact email, phone, and address.
- Confirm pricing, shipping, refunds, returns, cancellations, and supplier/fulfillment details are transparent.
- Confirm there are no fake testimonials, unrealistic guarantees, hidden subscriptions, or copied brand assets.
- Confirm checkout totals and terms are visible before payment.

## 10. Business Model and Financials
- Confirm the website matches business registration, bank, processor, supplier, and fulfillment documents.
- Confirm expected sales volume, average order value, refund exposure, and chargeback handling are documented.
- Confirm product pricing and shipping fees align with financial and fulfillment records.
- Confirm third-party supplier or fulfillment relationships are documented.
